=== the leak in tools/redact.py, repaired

Recorded by pc-rpgmaker2000-doc/docs/13 (correction 1) and again by
pc-rpgmaker2003-doc/docs/12. The tool that exists to remove routable
addresses carried a real one twice, in its own selftest fixtures, in
a committed and published file. Nothing in this note reproduces it:
the counts are the finding and the string is not.
